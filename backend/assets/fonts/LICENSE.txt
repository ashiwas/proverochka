The DejaVu fonts (DejaVuSans.ttf, DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf) are bundled here so the
КП (commercial proposal) PDF generator can render Cyrillic text and the ruble
sign. DejaVu fonts are released under a permissive free license (Bitstream Vera
+ Arev), which allows redistribution. See:
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
